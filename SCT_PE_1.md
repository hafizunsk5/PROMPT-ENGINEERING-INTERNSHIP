__TASK 01 – WRITING BETTER PROMPTS__

__Prompt 1 – Vague Prompt__

“Explain artificial intelligence\.”

Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) is the branch of computer science that focuses on creating machines and software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence\.

These tasks include:

- __Learning__ from data and experience\.
- __Reasoning__ to solve problems\.
- __Understanding language__ \(such as speech and text\)\.
- __Recognizing images and objects__\.
- __Making decisions__ based on available information\.

__Types of AI__

1. __Narrow AI \(Weak AI\):__
	- Designed to perform a specific task\.
	- Examples: Voice assistants, recommendation systems, spam filters\.
2. __General AI \(Strong AI\):__
	- A theoretical form of AI that can perform any intellectual task a human can do\.
	- It does not yet exist\.

__Applications of AI__

- Healthcare \(disease diagnosis, medical imaging\)
- Education \(personalized learning\)
- Banking \(fraud detection\)
- Transportation \(self\-driving vehicles\)
- Agriculture \(crop monitoring\)
- Customer service \(chatbots\)

__Advantages__

- Performs repetitive tasks quickly and accurately\.
- Works continuously without fatigue\.
- Improves decision\-making using data\.
- Increases productivity and efficiency\.

__Disadvantages__

- High development and maintenance costs\.
- May replace some jobs through automation\.
- Can produce biased results if trained on biased data\.
- Raises privacy and ethical concerns\.

__Prompt 1 – Improved Prompt__

“Explain Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) in simple language for a college student who is new to AI\. Give 5 key points, 3 real\-world examples, and a short conclusion\. Keep the explanation under 200 words\.”

__Artificial Intelligence \(AI\)__

Artificial Intelligence \(AI\) is a branch of computer science that enables computers and machines to __learn, understand, solve problems, and make decisions__ similar to human intelligence\.

__5 Key Points__

1. __Learning:__ AI learns patterns from data and improves its performance\.
2. __Problem\-solving:__ It can analyze information and find solutions\.
3. __Decision\-making:__ AI uses data to make predictions or recommendations\.
4. __Language understanding:__ It can understand and generate human language\.
5. __Automation:__ AI can perform repetitive tasks with little human effort\.

__3 Real\-World Examples__

- __Chatbots:__ ChatGPT can understand questions and generate answers\.
- __Recommendation systems:__ YouTube and Netflix suggest videos or movies based on your interests\.
- __Face recognition:__ Smartphones use AI to recognize faces and unlock devices\.

__Comparison & Analysis__

Comparison:  
The response to the vague prompt was more detailed and broad , covering types, applications, advantages, and disadvantages of AI\.

The improved prompt produced a more focused and organized response\. It followed specific instructions by providing 5 key points, 3 real\-world examples, and a conclusion\. The language was simple and appropriate for a college student who is new to AI\.

Analysis: Adding specific instructions about the audience, structure, and length made the AI response more focused and useful\.

__Prompt 3: Vague Prompt__

__Write about social media\.__

Social Media

Social media refers to online platforms where people can communicate, share information, create content, and connect with others\. Examples include Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp, Facebook, and LinkedIn\.

5 Key Points

1. Communication: Helps people stay connected with friends, family, and communities\.
2. Education: Students can access tutorials, courses, and useful information\.
3. Entertainment: Provides videos, music, games, and other content\.
4. Career opportunities: Platforms like LinkedIn help students build professional networks\.
5. Information sharing: News and ideas can spread quickly through social media\.

Advantages

- Easy communication
- Access to knowledge
- Networking opportunities
- Platform for creativity

Disadvantages

- Can waste time
- Misinformation can spread quickly
- Privacy and online\-safety concerns
- Excessive use can affect daily routines

__Prompt3 – Improved Prompt__

“Explain social media in simple language for a college student\. Give 5 important benefits, 5 common uses, and 3 tips for using social media responsibly\. Keep the explanation under 200 words\.”

__Social Media__

Social media is a group of online platforms where people can __communicate, share content, learn, and connect with others__\. Examples include Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp, and LinkedIn\.

__5 Important Benefits__

1. __Easy communication__ with friends, family, and communities\.
2. __Learning opportunities__ through educational videos and resources\.
3. __Career networking__ with professionals and organizations\.
4. __Information sharing__ about news, events, and ideas\.
5. __Creativity and expression__ through posts, videos, photos, and other content\.

__5 Common Uses__

- Chatting and video calling
- Watching educational or entertaining content
- Sharing photos and videos
- Promoting businesses and products
- Finding jobs and professional connections

__3 Responsible\-Use Tips__

1. __Manage your time__ and avoid excessive scrolling\.
2. __Protect your privacy__ by avoiding sharing sensitive personal information\.
3. __Check information carefully__ before believing or sharing posts\.

__Compare Prompt 3__

__Comparison:  
__The vague prompt produced a general explanation of social media, including its key points, advantages, and disadvantages\.

The improved prompt gave a more focused response with exactly 5 benefits, 5 common uses, and 3 responsible\-use tips\. It also used simple language suitable for a college student\.

__Analysis: __The improved prompt provided clear instructions about the audience, required information, structure, and length\. As a result, the AI response was more organized, specific, and relevant\.
